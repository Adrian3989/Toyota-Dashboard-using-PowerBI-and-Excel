# Toyota-Dashboard-using-PowerBI-and-Excel
In this project (which Language is in Spanish), I've created Dashboards using fictional data about Toyota, this is a personal project about Business Intelligence and Data Analytics. Each one of the dashboards belong to the Balanced ScoreCard Analysis, evaluating the 4 perspectives:
**1.Financial:** Measures economic performance, such as revenue growth, ROI, and cost efficiency.(Finanzas)
**2.Customer:** Evaluates customer satisfaction, loyalty, and market share.(Ventas)
**3.Internal Processes:** Focuses on operational efficiency, quality, and cycle times.(Producción)
**4.Learning & Growth:** Assesses employee training, culture, and technology infrastructure.(Empleados)
There are also 2 aditional Dashboards evaluating secondary information.

## Findings

### Sales:
• Over the last 5 years, 2022 saw the highest sales volume, with 2,013,300 units worldwide.
• Europe accounts for the largest percentage of sales, with 32.21% of the total.
• The United States has the highest sales volume over the last 5 years, leading in 2021, 2023, and 2024. Japan also has high sales, ranking second.
• SUVs are the best-selling model, representing 23.76% of total sales, followed by sedans (16.54%) and sports cars (13.28%).

### Finance:
• Global net profit is 166 billion, doubling costs (82 billion).
• Revenue has grown year after year, from 45 billion in 2021 to 55 billion in 2025.
• Asia is the continent that has generated the most profit, with 34.45% of the total.
• The United States again leads the list of countries with the highest profits (19 billion), followed by Japan (16 billion).

### Production:
• Production has increased year after year, from 21 billion in 2021 to 27 billion in 2025.
• The continent with the highest production is Europe (32.48%) with 38 billion.
• The SUV is the most produced car type over the last 5 years (23.03% of the total).

### Employees:
• The continent with the highest percentage of employees is Asia with 34.71% (6 million).
• Japan has the most employees, with approximately 2,055,000.
• The most common job title is Operator, while Managers are the least common.

## Conclusions

• The United States and Japan lead the list most of the time, being Toyota's most important markets. Other significant markets include Germany, Brazil, China, and South Korea.
• Although sales have remained at similar levels, production volume and revenue have increased over the last 5 years. This suggests that the brand holds a special place in its customers' preferences.
• By a wide margin, the most numerous employees are the Operators (Mechanics, Support, Maintenance). Therefore, they are Toyota's main human resource, and the company should prioritize and promote the development of their skills.
• SUVs are Toyota's biggest bet, as they represent the largest number of vehicles sold and generate more revenue than any other type of car.
